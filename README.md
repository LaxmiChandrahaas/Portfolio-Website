## ✨ Key Features

- **Glassmorphism UI** – Frosted glass cards with subtle neon borders and glow effects.
- **Animated Background Orbs** – Floating purple, cyan, and pink gradients that move dynamically.
- **Typed.js Integration** – Auto‑typing hero titles (AI Enthusiast · IoT Explorer · IEEE Leader).
- **Animated Stat Counters** – CGPA, research papers, certifications, and leadership roles count up smoothly on scroll.
- **Fully Responsive** – Seamless experience on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- **Downloadable Resume** – One‑click PDF download for recruiters.
- **Interactive Hover States** – Cards lift, scale, and glow, providing a premium feel.
